How The System Works

From initial citizen reporting to administrative verification and final resolution.

01

1. Submit Complaint

Citizens select the service category, specify the street location, and describe the problem with priority.

02

2. ID Generated

The Flask backend records the grievance into SQLite and generates an immediate, unique tracking ID.

03

3. Admin Action

Municipal administrators review details on the dashboard, assign resources, and advance the status to In Progress.

04

4. Resolved & Closed

Ground teams complete remediation. The ticket is marked Resolved, and citizens can verify the final outcome.
